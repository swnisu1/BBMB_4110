Enzyme Kinetics Fitting Tools

Interactive tools for fitting and exploring enzyme kinetics data. Everything runs in your browser; your data never leaves your computer.

Start here: How the Fitting Widgets Work

What the fitting tools do behind the scenes, and how to read their output.

Rates and time courses

Progress Curve Fitting

Fit whole time courses to the integrated Michaelis–Menten equation for kcat and KM.

Model Discrimination

Fit a decay with one or two exponentials and see when AIC and BIC justify the bigger model.

Substrate dependence

Plate Reader Kinetics

Load the plate reader .xlsx, get a slope for each well, convert ΔA340 to µM NADPH/min and fit MM, Hill or substrate inhibition.

Substrate Dependence Fitting

Fit initial rates to Michaelis–Menten, Hill or substrate-inhibition models; compare WT and mutants.

Two-Substrate Kinetics Explorer

See how the apparent KM for one substrate depends on the other; compare WT and a mutant.

Inhibition

Inhibition Mechanism Fitting

Fit competitive, uncompetitive, noncompetitive and mixed models; BIC picks the best.

IC50 Fitting

Fit dose–response curves to estimate IC50.

Lab calculators

DNA Molarity Calculator

Turn ng/µL and length into nM; get moles in a volume and the dilution to a target molarity.
