How it’s calculated

Molarity is mass concentration divided by molecular weight. Since 1 ng/µL = 1 g/L × 10−3:

molarity (M) = concentration (ng/µL) × 10⁻³ ÷ MW (g/mol)
molarity (nM) = concentration (ng/µL) × 10⁶ ÷ MW

Molecular weights use average masses of the sodium-free nucleotides:

  • dsDNA: MW = bp × 617.96 + 36.04 g/mol
  • ssDNA: MW = nt × 308.97 + 18.02 g/mol
  • ssRNA: MW = nt × 320.5 + 159.0 g/mol

Quick check: 50 ng/µL of a 3,000 bp plasmid is 50 × 10⁶ ÷ 1,853,916 ≈ 27 nM. For circular plasmids the end correction doesn’t apply, but at this size it changes nothing.
