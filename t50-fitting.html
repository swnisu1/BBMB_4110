Results

95% intervals come from 1000 bootstrap resamples: tubes are drawn with replacement within each temperature, and the unheated tubes are resampled too. With only two or three tubes per temperature the intervals are approximate and tend to be too narrow.

Residuals

Measured − fitted activity retained

Scatter around zero is consistent with the sigmoid. A run of points on one side suggests the curve shape or the plateaus do not match the data.

What is being calculated?

Activity retained

retained (%) = 100 × vheated / v̄unheated

Each heated tube is compared with the mean of the unheated tubes for the same series.

Interpolated T50

The mean retained activity is taken at each temperature. T50 is found by a straight line between the two neighboring temperatures where the mean drops from above 50% to below 50%. If the data never cross 50%, the T50 is reported as above or below the measured range.

Sigmoid

A(T) = bottom + (top − bottom) / (1 + e(T − Tmid)/w)

Tmid lies halfway between the fitted plateaus, and w sets how sharp the transition is. The temperature where the curve crosses 50% of the untreated activity is

T50 = Tmid + w · ln[(top − 50) / (50 − bottom)]

T50 and Tmid are equal only when the plateaus are 100% and 0%. State which value you report.

The sigmoid is fitted to every included tube by least squares on the retained-activity scale. With free plateaus, top and bottom are fitted; with the 100% to 0% option they are fixed. ΔT50 = T50(series) − T50(reference).
