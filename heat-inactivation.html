Residuals

Measured − fitted activity

Scatter around zero is consistent with the model. Curvature or a systematic trend suggests it is missing part of the time course.

What is being fit?

Single exponential

A(t) = A₀ e−kinactt

A₀ is the fitted activity after the heating ramp. kinact describes the fractional loss of activity per unit time at the challenge temperature.

t½ = ln(2) / kinact

The half-life is the holding time needed to lose half the post-ramp activity.

Exponential with a plateau

A(t) = A∞ + (A₀ − A∞) e−kt

A∞ is the activity remaining at the plateau. Use this model when late measurements support a persistent active fraction.

Here, ln(2)/k is the half-life of the decaying component above the plateau. At that time, A(t) = A∞ + (A₀ − A∞)/2.

Both models minimize squared residuals on the original activity scale, using every included replicate with equal weight. Negative blank-corrected measurements are retained. Fitted amplitudes and rate constants are constrained to be nonnegative. An unheated reference is not a time-zero fit point.
