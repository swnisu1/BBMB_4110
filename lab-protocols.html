BBMB 4110

Lab Protocols

Handouts for the elective experiments. Each opens as a Word document.

E2 Urea Unfolding

Compare WT and mutant unfolding by tryptophan fluorescence and fit Cm, m and ΔGwater.

E3 Temperature Dependence of Residual Activity

Heat samples across a thermocycler gradient for a fixed time and find T50.

E4 Time Dependence of Heat Inactivation

Hold samples at one temperature for different times and fit an inactivation rate and half-life.

E5 Kinetics of Ketone Reduction

Follow NADPH consumption to measure apparent kcat and Km for a ketone and for NADPH.

E6 Dependence of Activity on pH

Measure a pH-activity profile across overlapping buffers, fit apparent pKa values, and test recovery after exposure.

E7 Characterization of a Second Inhibitor

Fit a substrate-by-inhibitor matrix to find the inhibition pattern of a new compound and compare it with the R7 inhibitor.
