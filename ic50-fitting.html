IC50 Fitting

Fit dose-response data to get an IC50 (or a tight-binding Ki), convert it to Ki, and use how the IC50 shifts with substrate concentration to see which inhibition mechanism is at work. Assumes the cofactor is held at a saturating concentration.

Data source

Simulated experiment

Responses are % of the no-inhibitor rate at each [S]. The simulator uses standard kinetics with the inhibitor in large excess over the enzyme, so use it with the Hill model, not the tight-binding model.
