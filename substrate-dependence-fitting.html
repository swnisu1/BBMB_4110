Eadie-Hofstee plot

v against v/[S]. Michaelis-Menten data fall on a straight line, cooperativity bends the points into a hump, and substrate inhibition curls the high-[S] points back. It is a good way to see departures from a hyperbola but a poor way to get the constants.
