BBMB 4110 · Data Fitting Apps

New Fitting Apps

A new way to work: find the slopes once, save them in a rates file, and fit that file in any of the fitting apps as many times as you like. Loading the plate with its rates file restores your slope work, so nothing has to be redone. The original apps are still on the Data Fitting Apps page.

Step 1: slopes

Plate Reader Slopes

Load the plate reader .xlsx, check each well's slope, describe the plate ([S], [S₂], [I], enzyme, [E]), convert ΔA340 to µM NADPH/min and save a rates file.

Step 2: fit the rates file

Substrate Dependence Fitting

Michaelis–Menten, Hill or substrate inhibition; compare WT and mutants.

Inhibition Mechanism Fitting

Competitive, uncompetitive, noncompetitive and mixed inhibition; pools replicate plates.

Two-Substrate Fitting

Sequential, ping-pong and equilibrium-ordered mechanisms, with the double-reciprocal pattern.
