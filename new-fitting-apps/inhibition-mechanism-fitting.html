Model comparison

The widget does not choose a model for you. Compare the models, then click the row of the one the data support; the fit, residuals and constants are shown only for the model you choose (click the row again to clear it). BIC = n ln(SSE/n) + k ln(n); lower is better. The weight is the approximate probability of each model among those compared (Δ BIC above 10 is very strong evidence).
