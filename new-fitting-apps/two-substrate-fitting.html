Model comparison

The widget does not choose a mechanism for you. Compare the models (and the plots below), then click the row of the one the data support; its curves, residuals and constants appear (click it again to clear). BIC = n ln(SSE/n) + (k + 1) ln(n); lower is better. The weight is roughly the probability of each model among those compared.

Rate vs. [S]

Double reciprocal: 1/v vs. 1/[S]

Points are the means at each concentration; lines are the chosen model. Lines that intersect (to the left of the y axis) point to a sequential mechanism, where both substrates bind before any product leaves. Parallel lines point to ping-pong, where the first product leaves before the second substrate binds. Judge the pattern with the error in mind: lines that are nearly parallel are hard to tell from parallel.

Apparent constants at each [S₂]

A separate Michaelis–Menten fit to each curve. For ping-pong, Vmax,app/Km,app stays the same at every [S₂]; for a sequential mechanism it rises with [S₂].
